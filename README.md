# OECD Health & Spending Explorer

**COS30045 — Data Visualisation**

An interactive D3.js visualisation exploring the relationship between **health expenditure per capita** and **life expectancy at birth** across OECD countries.

## Project Aim

The project aims to help users explore questions such as:

- Is higher health expenditure associated with higher life expectancy?
- Which OECD countries spend the most on healthcare per person?
- Which countries have the highest and lowest life expectancy?
- How do health expenditure and life expectancy change over time?
- How does a selected country compare with other OECD countries?

## Data Sources

Data is sourced from **OECD Health Statistics**.

### Life Expectancy at Birth
- Annual life expectancy by OECD country
- Measured in years
- **Team member:** Andreana

### Health Expenditure Per Capita
- Annual healthcare expenditure per person
- PPP-adjusted values where available
- **Team member:** Nikki

The datasets will be combined using **country and year**.

## Proposed Visualisation

The current design includes:

1. **Scatter Plot**  
   - X-axis: Health expenditure per capita
   - Y-axis: Life expectancy
   - Each point represents an OECD country

2. **Trend Chart**  
   - Shows changes over time for a selected country

3. **Interactions**
   - Year filtering
   - Country selection
   - Hover tooltips
   - OECD average comparison
